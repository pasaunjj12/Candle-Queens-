Candle Safety Legal Notice

This Legal Notice applies to all candles and related products sold by Candle Queens.

Important Safety Information

All Candle Queens products include a warning label located on the bottom of the candle. Customers are responsible for reviewing and following all instructions prior to use.

To reduce the risk of fire, injury, or damage:

  • Do not burn candles for more than four (4) hours at a time.
  • Always trim the wick before each burn.
  • Keep candles away from flammable materials, including curtains, fabrics, and paper.
  • Burn candles on a stable, heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
